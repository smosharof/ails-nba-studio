Trainer

The Trainer agent keeps the recommendations current. Each week we record what was done for each HCP (a call, an email, a digital touch, or nothing) and then measure the outcome, the number of new prescriptions they wrote in the following two weeks. The Trainer checks whether the model has learned from the most recent weeks of those results, decides whether to retrain the model and whether to republish the recommendations, and shows its work. What it publishes is what the Recommender answers from.

Recommender

The Recommender agent answers questions about what the Trainer publishes: who to contact, through which channel, and why. It reads only the recommendation table, cannot change anything, and never writes its own product claims; messages come from pre-approved wording. For questions its fixed lookups do not cover, such as counts or comparisons, it writes a read-only query; the SQL is shown under the answer.
